### What the attacks changed that outlives the unit

- **Two address domains.** `ContentHash::of_bytes` wrote no tag and shared one space with
  `ContentHash::of`, so a payload whose bytes were some value's canonical encoding took that
  value's address — and `of_bytes` is the path the story names for observations and evidence,
  which are supplied from outside. Now `ekr.payload.v1` and `ekr.value.v1`, neither a prefix of
  the other. Every pinned digest moved once, for this reason and no other.
- **The acceptance could not fail.** It named a `Node` that lives in a crate this one must not
  depend on, so the fixture renamed its own field and compared a `Copy` id to a copy of itself; it
  passed with `mint()` returning a constant. Restated here as the half `ekr-core` can verify, and
  carried to `story:graph-model-and-assertions` as the half it cannot.
- **A guard that passed on prose.** The coverage check added in correction 1 was satisfied by any
  public item whose name is an English word; `Encoder::list` was untested and passed on the word
  "list" in a doc comment. It now requires a use, and the adversary's independent check agrees.
- **Two newtypes over one shape still share an address** — `task:canonical-newtype-discriminant`,
  blocking the story that first writes a root hash anybody keeps.
