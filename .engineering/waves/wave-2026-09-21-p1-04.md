## Coordinator work, in the opening commit

### 1. `Timestamp` is settled before dispatch, and not where the plan put it

`architecture-decision-record:0004-timestamp-in-ekr-core`, accepted: `Timestamp` is a newtype in
**`ekr-core`** over `i64` milliseconds since the Unix epoch, structural under `Canonical`, with a
decimal text form.

The plan said `ekr-graph` would define it. Reading the specification before dispatch overturned
that. `systems/ekr/domains/ontology.yaml:100-101` declares `SchemaVersion.created_at` of type
`Timestamp`, and `ekr-ontology` does not depend on `ekr-graph` — the order is
`ekr-core <- ekr-ontology <- ekr-graph <- ekr-store <- ekr-kernel`. A `Timestamp` in `ekr-graph`
would leave one ESS scalar carried by two unrelated Rust types, which is the drift
`crates/ekr-ontology/tests/domain_projection.rs` exists to catch. Four of the five domains declare
a `Timestamp` field, so the type belongs at the bottom of the stack.

The consequence is a scope extension the story did not have: `ekr-ontology` carries a bare `i64` at
three sites today (`value.rs:208`, `value.rs:149`, `schema.rs:31`) and adopts the newtype. Written
into the story's typed scope and its `## Scope` section before dispatch.

### 2. The acceptance holds as written

"A `GraphSnapshot` over the § 65 fixture (Alice `CEO_OF` Acme until 2026-03-12, Bob from then)
answers `active()` with Bob." A snapshot that answers `None`, or that answers Alice, fails it. No
restatement needed, unlike p1-03's and `story:transaction-and-validators`'s.

### 3. The mechanism the p1-03 decision deferred lands here

`task:canonical-newtype-discriminant` settled that sum types carry a variant tag and newtypes do
not. `crates/ekr-core/src/canonical.rs` states it as a contract and names `RevisionEvent` as the
first sum type that needs the writer. This wave adds `tag::VARIANT` and `Encoder::variant`, and the
task closes when they land with a caller.
