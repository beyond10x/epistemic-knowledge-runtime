## Coordinator work, in the opening commit

The plan front-loads four things that are cheaper now than later. All four are the coordinator's;
none is the implementor's.

### 1. `task:canonical-newtype-discriminant` is settled

**Sum types carry a required variant tag. Newtypes carry no discriminant, and that is the stated
contract rather than an open question.** The reasoning is on the task; the short form is that the
two halves have different blast radii. A sum type's variants collide when their payload shapes
match and nothing about position distinguishes them — `RevisionEvent` has seven variants and lands
in p1-04. A newtype's collision needs a *bare* newtype hashed at top level, and every artefact this
runtime content-addresses is a structured value whose field positions already distinguish it. A
per-type discriminant would cost a stable tag per type forever; a rename or a reordering of that
registry silently moves every address the type ever reached.

**It moves no recorded address.** Nothing in the workspace is a sum type today — `Option` already
carries its own tags — so the decision is free at this point and would not have been at p1-06,
where the graph, the store's folds and the kernel's validation hashes all rest on the encoding.

`crates/ekr-core/src/canonical.rs` rule 5 is rewritten from the open question to the settled
contract. The mechanism — `tag::VARIANT` and `Encoder::variant` — lands in p1-04 with
`story:graph-model-and-assertions`, which is scoped for it, because a helper with no caller is a
helper nobody has tested against a real shape. The task's `blocks` edge on
`story:commit-and-revision-lineage` is taken back: what blocked that story was the undecided
question.

### 2. Two acceptance statements that could not fail are restated

p1-02 spent a full adversary round on an acceptance that passed with `mint()` returning a constant.
Two of the four upcoming ones had the same defect, in mirror image, and both are fixed before
dispatch rather than after:

| story | was satisfied by | now |
|---|---|---|
| `story:ontology-types-and-values` | a checker compared against itself — "satisfies the declared type" is what `check` decides | "A value generated to violate exactly one property of its declared type is refused." The generator is the oracle |
| `story:transaction-and-validators` | `validate` answering `Err` to everything | "A transaction carrying exactly one of the five defects is refused, and the issue names that defect." |

### 3. The public-surface guard is lifted to the workspace

`crates/ekr-core/tests/public_surface.rs` scanned `CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR/src` — `ekr-core` alone — and
the other four crates had no `tests/` directory at all. From this wave onward every new public item
in `ekr-ontology`, `ekr-graph`, `ekr-store` and `ekr-kernel` would have arrived with nothing to
notice it was untested. `crates/ekr/tests/public_surface.rs` now scans every crate's `src/` against
every crate's `tests/` pooled, keeping the criterion the per-crate guard cost two adversary findings
to settle: comments stripped, and the occurrence must be a use rather than a bare word.

Verified rather than assumed: a `pub const fn` added to `ekr-ontology` and named by no case turns it
red with `["ekr-ontology::zarquon_probe"]`, and the tree is green without it.

**Deviation from the plan.** The plan also said to widen it to `pub struct`, `pub enum`, `pub trait`
and `pub type`. Reading the code showed the two halves cannot widen together: the use criterion is
`::name` or `.name`, and a type is used as `Type::…`, `&Type`, `: Type` or `Type {`, so widening the
declaration side alone would report every type in the workspace as untested. Filed as
`task:public-surface-covers-types` with that reason.

### 4. `AGENTS.md` § The gate now says what happens at N = 2

p1-05 runs two units at once. Cargo's exclusive lock makes concurrent compilation serialise rather
than corrupt, and unit artifacts are keyed by a hash including the manifest path — but `doc/<crate>`
is a single shared path and `task doc-check` from two trees writes the same files, and the uplifted
binary is one path. Sharing is not merely slow for concurrent work, it is unsound for `doc-check`.
The rule now reads: one tree shares, concurrent units get one each, and the wave page says so.
