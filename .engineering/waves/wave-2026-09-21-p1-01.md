`-parallel-safety`), recorded as `review-result:p1-<lane>-round-<n>`. Round 1: 13 findings on 10
stories, 13 outcomes `fixed`. Round 2: acceptance 4 findings, parallel-safety 1, design 1, scope
`approve`; all 6 round-2 findings `fixed` in the second revision. Per the rubric there is no third
round; the round-2 fixes stand unreviewed by a critic and are named as such.

The design finding of round 2 changed the crate map: `ekr-kernel` (ids, hashes) and `ekr-graph`
(types the validators and commit read) would have been a two-way Cargo dependency. A bottom crate
`ekr-core` now holds identity, hashing and canonical encoding; `ekr-kernel` sits above `ekr-graph`
and `ekr-store`. `docs/roadmap.md` § 3 carries the acyclic order. The wave-1 story therefore
creates six crates, not five.
