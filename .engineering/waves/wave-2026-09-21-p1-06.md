## Why this wave exists, and it is not in any story

An independent review on Fable over `56abc2b..f293b4d` found that **two of the three exit criteria
for this phase are carried by nothing**, and the coordinator had reported both as met.

| invariant | what the review measured |
|---|---|
| **1** — only a `ValidatedTransaction` commits | a commit lands at revision 1 in a process that **cannot link `ekr-kernel`**, on three hand-written events through the public `RevisionLog::append`. `ValidatedTransaction` has zero consumers in any `src/`. The store's own suite pins the negation green |
| **2** — a `Canonical → Transient` reference is unrepresentable at the type level | the sealed machinery is sound and **no field of `Node`, `Edge`, `Assertion` or `CanonicalGraph` has that type**. A canonical edge into a transient root compiles, constructs, and is refused by the kernel — the word the sentence excludes |

Both are the coordinator's, and the second sentence of invariant 1 was unimplementable as written:
`ekr-store` sits **below** `ekr-kernel`, so its writer cannot take a `ValidatedTransaction`, and a
sealed trait in the store excludes the kernel along with everybody else.

`architecture-decision-record:0007-the-commit-path-is-the-kernels` and
`…:0008-canonical-state-references-are-typed` settle both, accepted before dispatch.

## Correction: this table over-reached, and the implementor caught it

**Written below as four cases the unit must turn green. Two of them cannot be green, by
construction, and the coordinator should have seen it before dispatch.**

A case that *constructs* the forbidden value cannot survive a fix that makes the value
unconstructible. The run-time membrane case and the `trybuild` case both pass a bare `NodeId` into
`Edge::new` in the identical position, so **one compiles exactly when the other does** — and the
`trybuild` case failing to compile is the acceptance. The stale-commit case has the same shape one
level up: it needs a commit to land through the very path finding A closes, and it shares the file's
authority-less `seeded()` helper.

The implementor refused to edit either file, proved the contradiction, and left two exact patches.
The coordinator verified the argument against `Edge::new`'s new signature and applied both. Patch 1
renames the run-time case to what it can assert — canonical state resolves a reference it holds and
answers `None` for one it does not — and keeps the trybuild case carrying the membrane claim. Patch 2
adds a sixteen-line local authority to the stale case and **changes no assertion**.

**The governing records were right and this page was wrong.** ADR 0007's exit criterion is case A
alone; ADR 0008's is the trybuild case alone; neither task's `## Tests` lists the two demonstration
cases. The two real exit criteria are marked below.

## The `AGENTS.md` correction, and it is the coordinator's third wrong sentence in two days

`AGENTS.md:85-86` said unit artifacts are keyed by a hash including the manifest path, "so two
trees' `deps/` do not clobber each other". **Measured false.** The reviewer's probe: two
byte-identical crates of one name in two directories, one shared build directory — `deps/` holds
**one** binary for both, and the second directory's `cargo test` ran the first's in 0.01s and
reported the first's compile-time path.

So the clause is struck, the reuse it contradicted is stated with the clobber as its mechanism, and
`cargo clean -p` is recorded as a move rather than a fix. Counts corrected to **20 files, 39 uses**,
including `xtask/src/main.rs:32`, which is not a test.
