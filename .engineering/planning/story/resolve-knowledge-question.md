## Outcome

A trusted human chooses a supported claim, retracts an incorrect claim or corrects effective time. Validated correction resolves the relevant attention question while retaining the answer, evidence and history; uncertainty can remain unresolved.

## Acceptance

Named cases: human_resolution_preserves_evidence_and_history; changed_answer_basis_requires_review. Host authentication supplies operator identity; agent content cannot supply its own authority. Revalidate on store advancement: unrelated changes permit the same answer, changed evidence/options/effects require renewed review. Recompute all affected disputes. Expose CLI/SDK attention answer and read-only answer history. Both providers, reopen and replay.
