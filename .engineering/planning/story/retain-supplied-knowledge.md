## Outcome

Import observations independently of canonical revisions and retain immutable typed interpretations with local schemas, source observation references, blockers and processing receipts. Repeated import returns the original receipt without duplicate records. Unmappable knowledge and rejected interpretations' source bytes remain inspectable after reopen.

## Boundaries

Exact document coordinates and digest envelopes avoid self-referential hashes. Validate local interpretation shape without requiring canonical mapping. No polling, checkpoints, GC, general retention reclamation or canonical observation-evidence admission in this story.
