## Objective

From `atlas/ROADMAP.md` O6: every component records what it did; the system proposes, lands and
re-measures its own improvements.

## This repository's contribution

Failure to integrate is information (`design § 25`): repeated non-canonical structure produces a
schema proposal from evidence. Epistemic health is measured, not assumed (§ 61, § 75). Lessons and
gates are ordinary canonical nodes whose recurrence is a metric (`docs/predecessors.md` A9).

## Evidence that closes this contribution

The § 64 fixture: parked facts integrate after a schema version the runtime proposed; a metrics
surface that lists the § 61 counters; a recurrence count on a lesson that changed a gate.
