## Objective

From `atlas/ROADMAP.md` O2: rules, state and engineering method are declared as data and executed by
the component that owns them; a claim carries the evidence behind it.

## This repository's contribution

Every canonical assertion carries evidence (`design § 16`, invariant 6.5); every change to canonical
state is a validated transaction (§ 19–20) committed as an immutable revision (§ 34); the ontology
itself evolves only through schema transactions with stronger gates (§ 26, § 50). "An agent said so"
is not provenance.

## Evidence that closes this contribution

A canonical assertion whose `ekr explain` chain reaches an external observation (§ 62); a replay
from the seed that reproduces the current root hash; a schema change that exists only as a
`SchemaProposal` with its supporting evidence and migration plan.
